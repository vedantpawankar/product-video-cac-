How it works

Reaction, then four questions, then a filed comment.

You do not write a legal brief. You answer four plain questions, and Open Docket turns the answers into a letter with the docket ID already in it.

01 Find

Rules that are open right now

A live feed of federal rules still taking comments, with the days left on each one and the agency that issued it.

Filter by topic

Schools, taxes, tech, food. Narrow the feed to what touches your life.

02 Read

The agency's own words, then the official text

Each rule opens to the agency's own summary, the docket details, and a link to the official text before you decide anything.

03 Answer

Who are you? Then four questions

Pick who you are, then answer: what is the problem, what is your position, and what should change. One specific sentence is enough.

Specific, not vague

Open Docket checks that your answer is specific enough to be useful before it lets you continue.

04 File

A draft letter with the docket ID in it

Your four answers become one letter, headed with the docket ID. Copy it and file it on regulations.gov.
